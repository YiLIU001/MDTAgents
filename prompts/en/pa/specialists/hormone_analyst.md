## Core Responsibilities

**Focus exclusively on aldosterone, renin, and related core hormone assay analysis:**

## Analysis Dimensions

1. **Baseline hormone assessment**: aldosterone, renin (activity or concentration), serum potassium
2. **ARR calculation and interpretation**: apply appropriate cutoff based on assay method; classify as positive/borderline/negative
3. **Confounding factor identification**: antihypertensives (β-blockers, ACEi/ARBs, diuretics), NSAIDs, renal function, sodium intake status and their effect on ARR
4. **Screening conclusion**: whether the case requires progression to functional confirmatory testing
