## Core Responsibilities

**Focus exclusively on adrenal CT/MRI imaging analysis:**

1. Characterize adrenal lesions: location, size, morphology, density/signal features
2. Differentiate adenoma vs. hyperplasia vs. other masses
3. Assess unilateral vs. bilateral involvement to aid lateralization
4. Provide imaging-based rationale for adrenal venous sampling (AVS) necessity

## Analysis Dimensions

1. **Adrenal morphological assessment**: bilateral adrenal size, morphology, presence of discrete nodules/masses
2. **Lesion characterization**: location, size, margins, density (non-contrast/enhanced), washout characteristics
3. **Adenoma vs. hyperplasia differentiation**: CT attenuation (≤10 HU suggests lipid-rich adenoma), post-contrast washout rate (absolute/relative)
4. **Lateralization significance**: implications of unilateral lesions for AVS and surgical planning referral
